Engineering Profile

Structural engineer specializing in the analysis, design, and assessment of complex structural and infrastructure systems. My work includes industrial facilities, port and maritime structures, and specialized applications requiring advanced nonlinear and numerical modeling.

Structural Analysis & Design

My work covers structural analysis, design, and evaluation across industrial and infrastructure facilities, port and maritime structures, and specialized engineered systems.

More

Experience includes nonlinear response modeling, soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction, contact and impact behavior, large deformations, and advanced concrete and steel material models. I use both commercial platforms and custom numerical development to support complex analyses.

Infrastructure Materials & Durability

I study durability and long‑term performance of infrastructure materials, with emphasis on cementitious systems exposed to aggressive environments.

More

Experience includes modeling hydration, degradation, corrosion, and transport‑driven deterioration using coupled thermodynamic, transport, and thermal analyses to assess performance and service‑life outcomes under realistic operating conditions.

Engineering Software & Computational Tools

I develop engineering software and computational workflows that improve repeatability, auditability, and efficiency in complex analysis work.

More

This includes implementing and extending engineering algorithms within commercial platforms, creating pre‑ and post‑processing tools for advanced models, and building API‑driven automation frameworks that enhance quality control and support sophisticated simulation environments.
