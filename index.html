Engineering Consulting Profile

I work across structural engineering, infrastructure materials, and engineering software development, providing technically defensible analysis, modeling, and decision support for complex engineering problems.

Structures

I work on the analysis, design, and retrofit of complex structural systems, including buried, onshore, and offshore structures. My experience includes advanced finite element modeling of nonlinear structural response, soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction, contact and impact behavior, large deformations, and nonlinear material behavior in concrete and steel systems, supported by both custom numerical development and commercial analysis platforms.

Infrastructure Materials

I work on durability and long-term performance of infrastructure materials, with a focus on cementitious systems exposed to aggressive environments. My experience includes modeling hydration, degradation, corrosion, and transport-driven deterioration processes using coupled thermodynamic, transport, and thermal approaches to assess performance, risk, and service-life implications under realistic conditions.

Engineering Software

I develop engineering software and computational workflows that make complex analysis work more repeatable, auditable, and efficient. This includes collaborating with commercial software platforms to implement and extend engineering algorithms, building pre- and post-processors for complex models, and developing API-driven automation tools that add capability and improve quality control across simulation workflows.
