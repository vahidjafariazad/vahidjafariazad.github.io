Engineering Consulting Profile

I’m a PhD and licensed Professional Engineer working across structural engineering, infrastructure materials, and engineering software development. My focus is delivering technically defensible analysis, modeling, and decision support for complex engineering problems.

Publications: Google Scholar

Structures

I work on the analysis, design, and retrofit of complex structural systems, including buried, onshore, and offshore structures. My experience includes advanced finite element modeling of nonlinear structural response, soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction, contact and impact behavior, large deformations, and nonlinear material behavior in concrete and steel systems. This work draws on both custom finite element development (including OpenSees-based implementations) and extensive use of commercial analysis platforms to support engineering decisions in challenging conditions.

Infrastructure Materials

I work on durability and long-term performance of infrastructure materials, with a focus on cementitious systems exposed to aggressive environments. My experience includes modeling hydration, degradation, corrosion, and transport-driven deterioration processes using coupled thermodynamic, transport, and thermal approaches. These models are used to assess performance, risk, and service-life implications under realistic operating and environmental conditions.

Engineering Software Development

I develop engineering software and computational workflows that make complex analysis work more repeatable, auditable, and efficient.
