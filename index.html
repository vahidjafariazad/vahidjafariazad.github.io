Engineering Profile

Structural engineer with extensive experience in the analysis, design, and assessment of complex structural systems. My work spans industrial and infrastructure facilities, port and maritime structures, and specialized systems requiring advanced numerical modeling.

Structures

I work on structural analysis, design, and evaluation of complex systems across industrial and infrastructure facilities, port and maritime structures, and specialized structural applications. My experience includes nonlinear response modeling, soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction, contact and impact behavior, large deformations, and nonlinear material behavior in concrete and steel systems, supported by both custom numerical development and commercial analysis platforms.

Infrastructure Materials

I work on durability and long-term performance of infrastructure materials, with a focus on cementitious systems exposed to aggressive environments. My experience includes modeling hydration, degradation, corrosion, and transport-driven deterioration processes using coupled thermodynamic, transport, and thermal approaches to assess performance and service-life implications under realistic operating conditions.

Engineering Software

I develop engineering software and computational workflows that make complex analysis work more repeatable, auditable, and efficient. This includes implementing and extending engineering algorithms within commercial platforms, building pre- and post-processors for complex models, and developing API-driven automation tools that improve workflow quality control and support advanced simulation environments.

This site provides technical discussion and illustrative content. It does not offer engineering-of-record services and does not substitute for project-specific professional engineering judgment.
