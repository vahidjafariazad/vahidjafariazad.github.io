Structures, Materials & Software

Structural engineer focused on complex infrastructure systems—where nonlinear behavior, multiphysics coupling, and durable materials performance shape the decision. Work spans industrial facilities, port and maritime structures, and specialized applications that need advanced numerical modeling.

Structural Analysis & Design

Analysis, design, and evaluation of industrial, waterfront, and specialized structural systems, from code-based design through high-fidelity nonlinear simulation.

More

Experience includes nonlinear response, soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction, contact and impact, large deformations, and advanced concrete and steel material models—using both commercial platforms and custom numerical development.

Infrastructure Materials & Durability

Durability and long-term performance of cementitious systems in aggressive environments, grounded in coupled chemistry–transport modeling.

More

Work covers hydration, degradation, corrosion, and transport-driven deterioration through coupled thermodynamic, transport, and thermal analyses that connect mechanisms to service-life outcomes under realistic operating conditions.

Engineering Software & Computational Tools

Software and computational workflows that make complex analysis more repeatable, auditable, and efficient.

More

This includes extending algorithms in commercial platforms, building pre- and post-processing tools for advanced models, and developing API-driven automation that strengthens quality control in sophisticated simulation environments.
