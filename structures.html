Structures

My work in structures spans a wide range of analysis and design problems, from conventional structural systems governed by standard code provisions to complex structures requiring advanced modeling and nonlinear dynamic analysis. The common thread is an emphasis on understanding governing mechanisms, selecting an appropriate level of analytical fidelity, and interpreting results in a way that directly informs engineering decisions.

Selected examples below are generalized and presented in a non-proprietary manner.

Focus Areas

Design range: conventional to advanced analysis

Structural systems ranging from straightforward framing and component design to cases requiring nonlinear material behavior, contact/impact effects, large deformations, or nonlinear dynamic response.

Nonlinear response and system behavior

Nonlinear mechanisms in concrete and steel systems, including stiffness degradation, component interaction, load path changes, and decision-relevant response measures under extreme loading.

Soil–structure and fluid–structure interaction

Integrated modeling where foundations and surrounding media influence system performance, boundary condition sensitivity, and governing demand under seismic, operational, or environmental loading.

Performance-based evaluation and retrofit reasoning

Model-driven evaluation used to compare alternatives, identify governing mechanisms, and support retrofit decisions for existing structures and complex assets.

The goal is not “more modeling.” It’s making modeling reliable enough that decisions can be defended.

Methods & Tools

Structural problems are approached using a combination of engineering judgment, simplified bounding analyses, and detailed numerical simulation as appropriate for the decision being supported. In addition to conventional analysis and design workflows, I’ve developed pre- and post-processing tools and API-driven automation to support repeatable modeling, reduce manual error, and improve traceability of results.

Structural analysis and design platforms

Conventional modeling and design workflows using CSI software (SAP2000, ETABS, SAFE) for structural systems, load combinations, design checks, and results interpretation.

Advanced finite element analysis

High-fidelity nonlinear simulation using advanced finite element tools (e.g., Abaqus, LS-DYNA) for problems involving nonlinear material behavior, contact/impact, large deformation, and nonlinear dynamics.

Automation and model tooling

Development of pre-/post-processors and software API–based workflows to support parametric studies, model generation, result extraction, quality checks, and consistent reporting.

Validation-oriented workflow

Bounding cases, sensitivity studies, and cross-checks against expected behavior, with traceable assumptions and decision-relevant outputs.
