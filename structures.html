Structures

My structural work centers on complex infrastructure systems where modeling quality directly affects engineering decisions. This includes nonlinear structural response, performance-based evaluation, and integrated soil–structure interaction, with emphasis on transparency, validation, and sensitivity to governing assumptions. Much of this work has been performed in marine and port environments and in high-consequence facilities where robustness and traceability matter.

What I’m Known For

Nonlinear response and failure modes

Capturing governing mechanisms (material nonlinearity, contact, large deformation) and linking results to interpretable demand and capacity measures.

Work examples (in preparation): brief writeups with figures and notes.

Soil–structure interaction for system-level behavior

Integrated models that include structural and geotechnical components to understand load paths, boundary effects, and sensitivity under seismic or extreme loading.

Work examples (in preparation): model schematics, assumptions, and key findings.

Performance-based evaluation and retrofit reasoning

Nonlinear static and performance-based studies used to compare alternatives and identify practical retrofit levers that move the outcome.

Work examples (in preparation): sanitized retrofit logic and response summaries.

Validation-first workflows

A bias toward checks: simplified bounding models, sensitivity runs, and cross-validation against expected behavior before trusting “pretty plots.”

Work examples (in preparation): verification checks and sensitivity patterns.
Notes and examples will be shared at a technical level suitable for discussion and illustration, without client-identifying details.

Representative Context

Experience includes performance-based seismic evaluation and nonlinear modeling for marine and naval facilities, including studies where 3D soil–structure interaction and nonlinear concrete behavior were central to retrofit decisions, as well as port and terminal infrastructure work involving heavy-lift operational demands and system-level performance.

This site provides technical discussion and illustrative content. It does not offer engineering-of-record services and does not substitute for project-specific professional engineering judgment.
